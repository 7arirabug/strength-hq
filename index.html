Rules & progression
RPE and rest times

RPE 6 ≈ 4 reps left · 7 ≈ 3 · 8 ≈ 2 · 9 ≈ 1 · 9.5 ≈ 0–1. RPE 10 is maximal effort. Accessories stay at RPE 7 unless otherwise shown.

Main top sets: 3–5 min rest. Weighted dip/pull-up volume: 3–4 min. Squats: 3–5 min. Other compound: 2–3 min. Isolation: 60–120 sec. Intensive grip: 1–3 min. Light grip: 45–90 sec.

Warm up with 5–10 minutes of movement and 2–4 increasingly loaded sets before your main lifts.

Grip progression & safe intensity

Weeks 1–2: stable, repeatable grip loads. Week 3: progress when recovery allows. Week 4: strongest week; selective near-limit efforts only with good recovery. Week 5: about half the grip volume, no near failure. Week 6: easy grip or rest.

At the gym: Tuesday grippers, weighted hangs, pinches and optional farmer’s walks; Thursday finger-flexion curls; Saturday heavy grippers, pinches and finger-flexion curls. At home: Monday/Thursday wrist extensions, Tuesday/Saturday PVC pipe cupping. All use the same daily training date.

Hangs and pinches: build 15–25 seconds before adding resistance. Stop safely before slipping or technique breakdown. No tennis-ball squeezes or mandatory Saturday weighted hangs.

Main-lift progression and calibration

Only add load when technique, prescribed reps and RPE allow it. Pull-ups/dips typically increase 1–2.5 kg at a time. Monitor bodyweight for weighted moves.

First Tuesday calibration: try +17.5 kg × 5 after warming up and adjust to RPE 7; this is an assessment, not a mandatory target. Dip and squat loads are established in week 1.

Week 5 tests controlled heavy doubles/triples without forced reps. Week 6 reduces volume and intensity. Track work at equivalent repetitions and effort.

Recovery priorities

Protect weighted pull-ups, dips and squats first. First skip optional Friday climbing and Tuesday farmer’s walks, then reduce Saturday gripper / pinch volume and nonessential accessories. Reduce Saturday pulling only if necessary.

Finger, elbow or shoulder pain and declining performance warrant load adjustments. Maintain adequate sleep, hydration and approximately 1.6–2.2 g protein/kg/day.

Strength first. Balanced development second. Skills later. No dedicated calisthenics skills in Block 1.
